# TechCorp Solutions - Employee Handbook & Company Policies

Welcome to TechCorp Solutions. This handbook outlines our core operating policies, benefits, guidelines, and expectations. All employees are required to review this document and adhere to these guidelines.

---

## 1. Code of Conduct and Workplace Policies

### 1.1 Professionalism & Respect
TechCorp is committed to fostering a professional, respectful, and safe work environment. Discrimination, harassment, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated and may lead to immediate termination.

### 1.2 Office Hours & Core Working Hours
Standard working hours are **Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM** local time, inclusive of a 1-hour lunch break. 
- **Core Collaboration Hours**: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM. During these hours, all team members are expected to be available for meetings and synchronous discussions.

### 1.3 Work From Home (WFH) Policy
TechCorp follows a hybrid model:
- All employees are eligible for up to **2 remote working days per week** (WFH).
- Mondays and Thursdays are designated as **Mandatory In-Office Days** for all staff.
- You must schedule your WFH days with your team lead in advance via the HR portal.
- While working remotely, employees must maintain a reliable internet connection and be available on Slack/Microsoft Teams during office hours.

---

## 2. Leaves, Holidays, and Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 2.1 Paid Leave Categories
Employees are entitled to the following paid leaves per calendar year:
1. **Annual Leave (Vacation)**: 15 days. Accrued monthly at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Up to 5 unused annual leaves can be carried over to the next year.
2. **Casual/Sick Leave**: 10 days. Granted at the beginning of the year. Cannot be carried over or cashed out.
3. **Parental Leave**: 
   - **Maternity Leave**: 26 weeks of fully paid leave for female employees.
   - **Paternity Leave**: 4 weeks of fully paid leave for male employees.
   - Application must be submitted at least 8 weeks prior to the expected date.

### 2.2 National Holidays
TechCorp observes 10 national holidays per calendar year, which are published in the company calendar in December.

---

## 3. Reimbursements & Benefits

### 3.1 Medical Allowance & Insurance
TechCorp provides comprehensive health insurance covering:
- Inpatient hospitalization for the employee, spouse, and up to 2 dependent children.
- Annual health check-ups up to **$300** per family member, claimable via the HR expense portal.

### 3.2 Learning & Development (L&D) Budget
We encourage continuous growth:
- Every employee receives an annual personal L&D budget of **$1,500**.
- This can be used for certifications, technical courses, books, or attending industry conferences.
- Prior approval from your department head is required before purchasing.

### 3.3 Travel and Business Expenses
When travelling on official business:
- Flights: Economy class is standard. Business class is permitted only for flights exceeding 8 hours with approval from the VP.
- Lodging: Reimbursement is capped at **$180 per night** for domestic travel, and **$250 per night** for international travel.
- Meal Allowance (Per Diem): Cap of **$60 per day** for meals and incidentals.
